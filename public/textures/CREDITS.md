# Starter texture library

Color maps from [Poly Haven](https://polyhaven.com), re-encoded at 1024 × 1024.
All are CC0 1.0 (public domain): free to use, change and share, no credit required.
Authors and source pages are listed per texture in `index.json`.

## Saints Reborn logo wall

`saints_reborn_wall.jpg` is the one exception to CC0: it carries the Saints Reborn logo from
[Saints Reborn by Whompay](https://github.com/whompay/SaintsReborn) (Apache License 2.0), on a wall
background by 00xJS. New maps wear it on their outer walls by default.
